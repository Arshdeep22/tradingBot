## Summary
Fourteenth consecutive week of total failure with zero trades taken and zero P&L. The Supply & Demand Zone strategy is completely non-functional — it has not produced meaningful trade signals for over three months. The bot is effectively offline. The regime has been trending_down for 6 of 7 days, and the current strategy architecture cannot generate entries in trending regimes. This is no longer a parameter tuning problem; the entire strategy framework must be replaced or fundamentally restructured.

## Regime Assessment
Regime detection appears to correctly identify trending_down conditions (6/7 days). However, regime detection is actively harmful because the bot has no strategy capable of trading trending regimes. The regime detector works but the strategy routing layer is broken — it maps all regimes to a single strategy that only works in ranging markets.

## Structural Recommendations

**[HIGH]** REPLACE primary strategy: Implement a trend-following strategy (e.g., EMA crossover with pullback entries, or breakout-continuation) as the primary strategy when regime is trending_up or trending_down. Supply & Demand Zones should only activate in ranging/consolidation regimes.
  Expected: Generate 3-8 trades per week instead of 0. Even at 45% win rate with 2:1 RR, this produces positive expected value vs current zero activity.

**[HIGH]** Implement strategy-regime routing: Create explicit mapping — trending_up → long momentum/breakout strategy, trending_down → short momentum/breakdown strategy, ranging → Supply & Demand Zones. Currently all regimes route to one strategy that requires consolidation zones.
  Expected: The bot would have a viable strategy for every market condition instead of being dead 90%+ of the time. Expected to increase weekly trade count from 0 to 5-15.

**[HIGH]** Add a forced minimum activity threshold: If no trade signal is generated by 1:30 PM, relax filters by 30% automatically. If no trade by 2:30 PM, log a diagnostic dump of why every candidate was rejected. After 3 consecutive zero-trade days, trigger emergency parameter relaxation.
  Expected: Prevents multi-week dead periods. Provides diagnostic data to understand signal generation failures. Expected to produce at least 1-2 trades per week minimum.

**[MEDIUM]** Add VWAP-based mean reversion or opening range breakout as a secondary intraday strategy that does not depend on multi-day zone formation. These strategies generate signals purely from intraday price action.
  Expected: Provides trade opportunities independent of the zone-building process that has been failing for 14 weeks. Expected 2-5 additional signals per day.

**[HIGH]** Audit the zone detection code for bugs: 14 weeks of near-zero signals across all market conditions strongly suggests a code defect, not just poor parameter fit. Specifically check: zone freshness/expiry logic, price proximity threshold calculations, and whether zones are being built but immediately invalidated.
  Expected: May reveal a single bug responsible for the 14-week drought. If found, could immediately restore normal operation.
